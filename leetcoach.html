The problem

When you get stuck on a LeetCode problem, you have two options: stay stuck, or read the full solution. Neither teaches you much. What's missing is the middle ground a good tutor gives you: just enough of a push to keep thinking.

How it works

Every problem has a four-level hint ladder. You only climb as far as you need.

  1. Guiding question. A question that points you toward the right way to think about it.
  2. Named pattern. The technique to reach for, like two pointers or a hash map.
  3. Applied reasoning. How that pattern fits this specific problem.
  4. Pseudocode. The last resort, the structure without the finished code.

If the hints still aren't enough, a chat fallback lets you ask follow-up questions. Progress is tracked per problem, including how many hints you needed, so you can see what's sticking.

Under the hood

  • Flask backend with a REST API layer for problems, hints, and progress.
  • SQLite for user progress and hint usage.
  • Hint generation through the Anthropic API, with prompts designed per hint level.
  • Built with AI-assisted development (Claude Code), deployed on Railway.

Business model

  • Free: the first 10 problems with unlimited hints, then 4 free hints a day.
  • Pro: unlimited hints for $10/month or $100/year.
  • Career centres: group licenses for universities.
